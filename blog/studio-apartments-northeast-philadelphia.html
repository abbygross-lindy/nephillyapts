Studio Apartments in Northeast Philadelphia Under $1,300

July 14, 2026
Northeast Philly Apts › Blog › Renter Tips

A studio is the most affordable way into Northeast Philadelphia — and in a market where a Center City studio can top $1,600, NE Philly studios deliver real space for hundreds less. Here's where to find them.

Why a studio in Northeast Philadelphia makes sense

Studios are ideal for renters who want their own place without a roommate or a big rent bill — students, young professionals, downsizers, and anyone prioritizing budget and location. In Northeast Philadelphia, that trade-off is especially favorable: you get more square footage, easier parking, and quick access to Pennypack Park and Roosevelt Boulevard, all for less than a cramped studio closer to Center City.

Fountain Gardens: the portfolio's only studios

Fountain Gardens on Welsh Road is the one community in our Northeast Philadelphia portfolio with studio apartments, which makes it the most accessible entry point in the neighborhood. Studios run 815 square feet — larger than many one-bedrooms elsewhere in the city — with pricing that starts around $1,264. That's a number that's become almost impossible to find in Philadelphia for a well-managed building.

The community sits directly next to a public park with tennis and basketball courts, and Pennypack Park is steps away. Each building has elevator service, a landscaped courtyard with a fountain, and an on-site gym. It's pet-friendly, and free heat, hot water, and cooking gas are included — which keeps the true monthly cost close to the rent number.

What a studio really costs here

With heat, hot water, and gas included, a Fountain Gardens studio keeps your all-in monthly cost predictable — no surprise winter heating bills. Add free parking (versus $200–300/month in Center City) and the effective savings over a downtown studio add up fast. For renters watching every dollar, that bundle is often the difference between stretching and staying comfortable.

How to tour

Studios are the most in-demand and most affordable layout, so they lease quickly. See current availability, pricing, and photos on the Fountain Gardens page, or read more about living near Pennypack Park.
