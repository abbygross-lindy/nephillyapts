About Fountain Gardens

Fountain Gardens is the only one of our Northeast Philadelphia communities with studio apartments, which makes it the most accessible entry point in the neighborhood. The community sits directly adjacent to a public park with tennis and basketball courts, and Pennypack Park is steps from the building.

Each building has elevator service, welcoming lobbies, and lighted hallways. The landscaped courtyard with a fountain gives the community a calm, park-like feel that residents return to year after year. The on-site gym is in the building, not across a parking lot.

Residents consistently describe Fountain Gardens as the kind of place where neighbors know each other's names and hold the elevator. If you are looking for a well-run, well-maintained community at a price that actually makes sense, this is worth a tour.
