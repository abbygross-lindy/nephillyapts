Common Questions

What neighborhoods are these apartments in?▼
Our apartments are in Rhawnhurst, Far Northeast Philadelphia, and the Pennypack neighborhood — three of the most established residential communities in Northeast Philly. Each is served by SEPTA bus routes and sits within a short drive of I-95 and Roosevelt Boulevard.
Are these apartments pet-friendly?▼
Yes — all four communities welcome cats and dogs. Breed restrictions and pet fees vary by property. Assistance animals, including service animals and emotional support animals, are not pets: they are exempt from pet fees, pet rent, and breed restrictions. Contact the leasing office for specific details.
How far are these apartments from Center City?▼
Depending on the property, you are looking at a 25–35 minute drive. By transit, Boulevard Direct runs to Frankford Transit Center, where the L takes about 25 minutes into Center City.
Are there studio apartments available?▼
Yes — Fountain Gardens on Welsh Road offers studios, making it our most affordable entry point. One- and two-bedroom apartments are available at all four properties. Joshua House also offers three-bedroom units.
How do I schedule a tour?▼
Click the Schedule a Tour button on any property page to connect with our leasing team directly.
